Splash Adventure

Dive into 10 ocean zones, catch 60 amazing sea creatures, and build your dream aquarium!

How to Play

🤿

Dive & Explore

Grab your joystick and swim through beautiful ocean zones — from sunny coral reefs to mysterious frozen caverns.

🎣

Catch Fish

Swim up to sea creatures and hold your net steady. Rare fish are harder to catch — upgrade your gear to land legendary finds!

🐟

Feed & Grow

Place fish in your aquarium tanks, feed them, and watch them grow from tiny babies to majestic kings.

🏆

Collect & Compete

Fill your collection, earn gold from visitors, unlock upgrades, and climb the Game Center leaderboards!
